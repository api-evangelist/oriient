Online, retailers can measure almost everything and optimize nearly every interaction. They’ve built streamlined digital experiences that guide shoppers, grow baskets, and improve inventory management behind the scenes. Yet the moment shoppers and associates enter the store, much of that valuable context disappears, precisely where retailers need it most.
