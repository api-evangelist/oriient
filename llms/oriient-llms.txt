# Oriient

> Oriient provides software-only, hardware-free indoor positioning ("indoor GPS")
> and navigation delivered as a SaaS SDK. Its geomagnetic technology reaches
> roughly 1-meter (3-foot) accuracy without Wi-Fi, beacons, or installed
> infrastructure, and powers product-finding, order picking, visitor navigation,
> asset tracking, and in-store analytics for retail, grocery, and smart-building
> customers across thousands of locations. Oriient New Media Ltd is
> headquartered in Tel Aviv and is a Techstars portfolio company.

This is a catalog-generated llms.txt for the API Evangelist network. Oriient does
